How to Classify Your Inquiry

To assist our team in routing and responding to your email efficiently, we highly recommend defining the nature of your inquiry in your email subject line. 1. Technical Support and Bug Reports

While we painstakingly test MeluaPlay across popular browsers like Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge, the sheer diversity of browser versions, operating systems, hardware combinations, and video encodings means you might occasionally run into a glitch. If a certain video link refuses to play despite being a supported format (MP4, MKV, M3U8, etc.), or if a UI element misbehaves on your mobile device, please hit us with a bug report.

When reporting a technical bug, please use the subject line [Bug Report] Let us know the issue. To help us reproduce and squash the bug swiftly, kindly include:

  • The operating system you're currently using (e.g., Windows 11, iOS 16, Android 13).
  • The web browser and its approximate version (e.g., Google Chrome v118).
  • The specific URL of the video that failed to play (if applicable and safe to share).
  • A step-by-step descriptive summary of the actions you took prior to encountering the bug.
  • Screenshots illustrating any error messages or visual anomalies, if possible.

2. Feature Requests and Suggestions

MeluaPlay has dramatically evolved based directly on the feedback provided by our user base. We initially launched with basic capabilities, growing into a powerhouse handling DASH and HLS streams thanks strictly to community insight. If you possess a fantastic concept that you believe will enhance the playback experience or make MeluaPlay more accessible to diverse demographics, we truly want to hear it.

When suggesting new capabilities, please utilize the subject line [Feature Request] Brief description of the idea. Lay out explicitly how the suggested feature would function and, most importantly, the specific problem it would eradicate for you or other regular viewers. While we naturally cannot implement every proposition due to technological or resource constraints, every single suggestion is reviewed during our developmental roadmap meetings.

MeluaPlay draws a vast aggregate of daily traffic comprising users highly invested in multimedia, technology, streaming, and digital entertainment. Should you represent a brand, tech startup, or ad network wishing to deploy campaigns on our platform, we welcome collaboration.

For commercial intentions, please structure your subject line as [Partnership/Advertising] Brand Name. In the email body, explicitly specify your marketing goals, desired advertising real estate (e.g., ad-slot-left, ad-slot-bottom), estimated duration of the campaign, and realistic budgetary approximations. We pride ourselves on crafting mutually beneficial custom agreements tailored appropriately to your campaign’s specific metrics and demographic targets.

4. DMCA and Copyright Inquiries

It is imperative to note that MeluaPlay functions purely as a client-side video interface. We do not maintain any servers harboring video files, nor do we host, upload, or syndicate copyright-protected material. Our player fundamentally acts as a magnifying glass, locally rendering data from URLs provided independently by the user. Because we lack access to the originating servers where these files reside, we cannot 'takedown' content fundamentally.

However, we take digital rights management (DRM) and intellectual property severely. If you hold copyrights to specific materials and require guidance regarding how these streams function independently of our site, you may email us using the subject line [DMCA Inquiry]. Be advised that legally binding takedown notices must be directed to the actual storage hosts (e.g., Google Drive, AWS, independent file lockers) rather than our player utility, as we hold absolutely zero control over external server contents.
